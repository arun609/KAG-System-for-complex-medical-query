✅ 2. Scope of the Project
✔ In-Scope

Your system will:

Accept complex medical queries
Example:
"How is BRCA1 mutation related to breast cancer risk, and what drugs are used in treatment?"

Retrieve relevant biomedical knowledge
Entities: diseases, drugs, genes, symptoms, pathways.

Perform multi-hop reasoning
e.g., Gene → Disease → Drug

Generate an answer grounded in the knowledge graph
Not hallucinated.

Provide an explainable chain-of-thought
Example:

BRCA1 → associated with → Breast cancer

Breast cancer → treated by → PARP inhibitors

Use PrimeKG as the knowledge source.

❌ Out-of-Scope

Your system will NOT:

Diagnose real patients

Generate medical advice

Replace doctors

Train large LLMs from scratch

Perform real-time clinical decision making

This keeps your project safe, ethical, and feasible.
